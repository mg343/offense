The Golden Dome is a land-to-air missile defense system in place in countries across the world, notably Israel, which uses a complex system of projectile localization and trajectory estimation to determine the path of foreign devices entering a protected airspace, and then deploys defenses (what I can only assume are either massive rounds or some other specialized anti-drone materials) in a rapid fire fashion to intercept and destroy these enemies.
Copper Dome is a rudimentary version of this - scaled significantly down, and applied in a much more "friendly" setting - the living room. As it stands (subject to change**), the Copper Dome involves a central defense deployment turret and a few external monitoring devices, mapping to the primary dome defense and sattelites used for tracking incoming objects. The purpose, however, is to use lasers/small rounds to intercept... mosquitos! Conceptually, everything stays the same from the Golden Dome, just a much smaller scale.
